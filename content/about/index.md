I work in platform and SRE — Kubernetes, CI/CD, observability, and infrastructure for blockchain nodes and data-availability layers. Most of what ends up here started as a note to myself while debugging something in production: why a retry policy made an outage worse, why autovacuum fell behind on a busy table, why a client needed rewriting in a different language to hit its latency budget. I write from the EU (CET).

This blog also holds older notes from when I was learning Rust and poking at LLM tooling — left up for anyone who finds them useful, but not what I'd point you to first. Start with [Posts](/posts/) or [Tags](/tags/) if you want the current focus.
